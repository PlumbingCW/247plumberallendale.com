What hydro jetting does, in plain terms

A jetter pushes water down a hose to a nozzle with several small openings. Some face backward, and the water leaving them pulls the nozzle forward along the pipe. Others spray at an angle and scour the wall as it passes. The loosened material is carried ahead of the water to the cleanout or the sewer.

Two ideas matter here. Pressure is the force of the water at the nozzle. Flow is how much water moves per minute. Pressure cuts and strips. Flow carries the debris away. Good work balances both and picks a nozzle for the pipe size and material, rather than turning everything to maximum.

A cable cuts a channel and leaves the pipe wall coated. A jetter cleans the wall itself, which is why grease and scale tend to stay away longer afterward. We will not promise a number of years, because that depends on what goes down the drain after we leave.

When we will say jetting is not right

Water at jetting force can make a weak pipe worse. Old clay or cast iron with failed joints, a section that has settled out of line, or a pipe the camera shows cracked is not a pipe to blast. Orangeburg, the tar-paper pipe used in some mid-century homes, can deform under force.

In those cases we pull the nozzle back and run the camera first. If the picture shows damage, the better answer is sewer line repair or lining, and we explain what we saw instead of selling jetting anyway. A cleaning that leaves a collapsed pipe in place only moves the next backup further along.

Allendale: township sewer, rural septic, and what jetting reaches

Much of Allendale Township is on the township sewer, which runs about 50 miles of main and 8 lift stations to the Water Resource Recovery Facility. Our jetting reaches your house line, the pipe from your foundation toward the street connection. It does not reach the township main. If several homes on your block are backing up, ask the township utility where its responsibility ends, and see sewer line cleaning for how we separate the two.

Farmland in the south and west is mostly on private septic under the Ottawa County Health Department. There, jetting clears the line between the house and the tank, which is separate from pumping the tank itself. Homes around Jenison skew older (median year built 1973 for the CDP), so scale and root intrusion show up more often in jobs there.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Allendale?

Yes, we answer 24/7. A main line that stops at midnight is not a Monday problem. We confirm the pipe's condition first, and we price the work before it starts, including after-hours.

Is hydro jetting safe for my pipes?

It is safe on sound pipe when the nozzle and force suit the pipe. It is the wrong tool for cracked, collapsed or badly offset pipe, which is why we look first when there is any doubt.

What is the difference between jetting and a drain cable?

A cable cuts through a blockage and leaves a channel. Jetting washes the wall. If you want the cable side explained, our drain cleaning page covers snaking and cabling.

Can jetting remove tree roots?

With a root-cutting nozzle it can clear roots out of the line. Roots grow back toward the water, so the lasting fix is repairing the joint they came through.

Will you jet the township sewer main?

No. We clear your side of the connection, the house line. For the township's main, ask the township utility where its responsibility ends.

I am on septic. Can you jet my line?

We can clear the line between the house and the tank. Pumping the tank is a separate trade, so ask when you call and we will tell you honestly which problem it sounds like.

How long does a jetting visit take?

It depends on pipe length, how packed the blockage is and whether a camera pass is needed. We give you our expectation before we begin.

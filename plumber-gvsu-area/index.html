The campus and the housing around it

GVSU was established in 1960 on about 1,280 acres between Lake Michigan Drive and Pierce Street, east of 48th Avenue. The university's own fall 2026 snapshot counts 21,382 students across all its campuses, and it does not give an Allendale-only figure.

The township Master Plan reports that group quarters, mostly dorms, held 17.5% of residents in 2020. Wikipedia, citing township assessing records, counts about 8,000 off-campus student beds as of 2021. We treat that as an estimate.

The Master Plan also sets a goal of keeping new student-oriented apartments from extending west of 52nd Avenue. That is why the dense rental housing clusters toward campus. Campus buildings are run by the university, so this page covers off-campus houses and apartments only.

What goes wrong in shared and rented homes

These are patterns in how rentals are used, not claims about specific buildings.

One drain, many users.

A shared house sends a lot of kitchen and bathroom waste down one line. Grease, hair and wipes build up. Three clogs in a term point to drain cleaning plus a camera look rather than another quick snake.

Toilets that run or clog.

Wipes and heavy use shorten the life of flappers and fill valves. Toilet repair is often a parts swap, but repeat clogs deserve a drain check.

A tank heater serving a full house.

Back-to-back showers test a heater's capacity. Consider water heater repair, or sizing at replacement, when tenants report lukewarm water.

Empty units over the summer.

Water that sits can leave traps dry and faucets dripping unnoticed. Run every fixture before move-in and look under sinks for slow leaks.

Township rental registration, in plain terms

Allendale requires every residential rental to be registered with the township by March 31 each year. The fee is $10 per unit. Owners then hold a Certificate of Compliance, and units are inspected every four years, or every two years if the last inspection failed.

The township page does not list plumbing requirements, only a general interior and exterior inspection. Ask the Rental Housing office at 616-895-6295 what an inspector checks. A leak, a dead water heater or a backed-up drain is easier to fix before the inspection date than during it.

Source: Allendale Charter Township rental housing page.

Landlords: a short checklist

Know where each unit's main shutoff and floor drain cleanout are, and write it on the lease packet. Keep the date of every clog and every water heater service. Add a card by each toilet that says paper only.

Multi-unit buildings sometimes need work that goes beyond a single house. Our commercial plumbing page covers larger properties. Before buying a rental, ask for the repair history on the water heater and the main drain. Michigan licenses plumbers through LARA's Bureau of Construction Codes, and its online license lookup lets you check any plumber before you hire one.

Tenants: who to call first

Read your lease. Most leases send repair requests to the landlord or property manager, who usually arranges and pays for them. Photograph standing water and note when the problem started. If water is spreading and no one answers, close the nearest shutoff and report it in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work on university-owned buildings?

This page covers off-campus houses and apartments. GVSU runs its own campus buildings, so ask the university about those.

Who pays for a plumbing repair in a rental?

That depends on the lease and on what caused the damage. Most leases route repairs through the landlord. Read yours and keep records of when you reported the problem.

How many students rent off campus in Allendale?

No single official number exists. Wikipedia, citing township assessing records, gave about 8,000 off-campus beds as of 2021, and group quarters held 17.5% of residents in 2020.

Does Allendale require rental inspections?

Yes. The township registers rentals annually and inspects them every four years, or every two years after a failed inspection. Contact the Rental Housing office for current rules.

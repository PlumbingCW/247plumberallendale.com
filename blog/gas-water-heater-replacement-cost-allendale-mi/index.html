Start with the answer: five things decide the price

The price of a gas water heater replacement comes from the tank itself, the venting, the gas and water connections, the work to remove the old unit and any permit. Most of the spread between two quotes lives in the venting and the extras, not in the tank.

We do not print dollar ranges, because no verified local pricing exists and a number out of context misleads. What you can do is ask for the clear price before work starts, in writing, with each of the lines below listed.

Allendale, MI: gas heat, Grand Rapids water and where permits go

The Census Bureau's American Community Survey (2020 to 2024) counts 5,900 Allendale Township households heating with utility gas, against 1,651 with electricity. That tells you gas service is common here, but it counts home heating fuel, not water heaters. If you are weighing a switch from gas to electric, ask your installer to price it separately.

The township buys water from the City of Grand Rapids, whose 2025 plant-tap average hardness is 142 mg/L, or 8.3 grains per gallon, with a range of 7.4 to 9.6. Allendale's own report prints no hardness number, so test your tap. Hard water leaves scale on the bottom of a tank, so ask about flushing and the anode rod when you choose a heater.

Professional Code Inspections handles plumbing and mechanical permits and inspections for the township. Ask your installer whether your job needs a permit and who pulls it. Michigan's plumbing code currently adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code, so ask your inspector which edition applies.

A quick swap versus a proper replacement

Both can finish the same day. The difference is what gets checked on the way.

A quick swap skips

  • Looking at the vent for gaps, corrosion or poor slope.
  • Replacing an old gas connector and valve.
  • Checking for water or gas leaks after connecting.
  • Asking about a permit or inspection.

A proper replacement includes

  • A vent check and a test for proper draft.
  • New connectors where the old ones are worn.
  • A leak check at every joint, including the gas side.
  • A clear explanation of the permit and what happens next.

How to compare two gas water heater quotes

Ask for each quote to list the same items. Then look at these five things, because they are where two prices usually differ.

Insurance rarely pays for replacing a worn-out water heater. Sudden events, such as a tank failure that floods a finished basement, are the usual covered kind, but wear and routine replacement typically are not. Check your own policy.

Tank and efficiency.

A larger tank or a higher-efficiency unit costs more up front and may save on gas later. Ask what the savings assumption is.

Venting changes.

Reusing a sound vent is simpler than a new run or a power vent. This is the biggest swing factor.

Access and removal.

A tank at the bottom of a narrow staircase takes longer to move than one in an open utility room.

Code and permit items.

Gas connections, relief valve piping and any permit work belong on the quote as separate lines.

After-hours timing.

A tank that fails in the middle of the night is priced differently from a planned swap. Ask for both.

Replace now or wait? What to weigh before you call

A tank that leaks from the body cannot be repaired and should be shut off. Close the cold water inlet, turn the gas control to off if you can reach it safely, and call. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911, and do not touch switches or the heater.

A heater that works but is old, rumbling or slow is a planning question. Replacing on your schedule usually means more choices than replacing in an emergency. See our water heater replacement and water heater installation pages for how those visits run.

Frequently asked questions

Why do two gas water heater quotes differ so much?

Usually because of venting, connectors and extras that one quote includes and the other leaves out. Ask each to list venting, gas and water connections, removal and permit work, then compare the lists.

Is a gas water heater replacement a DIY job?

We do not recommend it. It involves gas piping, venting and combustion air, and a mistake can cause a leak or carbon monoxide. If you smell gas, leave and call your gas utility first.

Does insurance cover a failed water heater?

Rarely for plain wear. Sudden events such as a tank that bursts and floods a finished space are the usual covered kind. Your own policy decides, so check it.

Can I switch from gas to electric?

Often yes, but it changes the electrical load and removes the vent. Ask for that option quoted separately so you can compare it with a like-for-like gas swap.

How long does a replacement take?

It depends on access, venting and whether any permit work is needed. We confirm the plan when you call, and same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows.

What happens if my heater fails at night?

Turn off the cold water to the tank if it is leaking, then call the 24/7 line. We will explain what to shut off and what to leave alone while you wait.

Does hard water matter for an Allendale water heater?

The Grand Rapids system reports a 2025 plant-tap average of 142 mg/L, 8.3 grains per gallon, and Allendale buys its water from that system. Scale can build up in tanks, so ask about flushing and test your own tap to see what your water is doing.

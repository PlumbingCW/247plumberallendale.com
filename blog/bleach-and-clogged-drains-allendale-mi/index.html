Bleach and Clogged Drains: An Allendale Homeowner Guide

Bleach does not clear a clog. It can whiten and disinfect the surface of a drain, but a mass of hair, grease or paper stays right where it is. Poured on top of another cleaner, bleach can also make a gas that hurts your lungs.

Below you will find what bleach really does inside a pipe, the mixing warning stated plainly, how septic tanks fit in, and a safe order for a clogged drain.

Why bleach doesn't unclog anything

A clog is physical. It is a mass of hair, soap film, food or wipes that blocks the pipe. Bleach is a disinfectant and stain remover. It reacts with some organic material, but it does not dissolve hair or break up a wad of paper in any way you can count on.

What you usually get is a drain that smells like bleach, still holds water, and now has a chemical sitting in the trap. When the plunger goes in, that liquid comes back up at you.

Bleach does have a job: wiping down a basin, a tub or the rim of a drain opening for mildew. Used that way, at the strength the label gives, it stays in its lane.

Warning.

Never mix bleach with other cleaners: toxic gas can result

Bleach combined with ammonia, with acids or with drain cleaners can release toxic gas. It can happen inside a drain, a toilet bowl or a bucket, and faster than you can react. There is no safe small test.

  1. Do not add bleach to a drain that already has any other product in it, even if that product went in days ago..
  2. Do not follow a drain cleaner with bleach, or a toilet cleaner with bleach, hoping to boost it..
  3. Do not combine bleach with ammonia-based glass or floor cleaners, vinegar, or any acid-type bathroom product..
  4. If you smell sharp fumes or your eyes and throat burn, leave the area, get fresh air and call emergency services or poison control..

What bleach does and what a clog needs

Bleach in the drain

  • Disinfects and lightens surfaces.
  • Does not remove hair, grease or paper.
  • Leaves a chemical sitting in standing water.
  • Creates a gas risk if anything else is in the line.
  • Large or repeated amounts can harm septic tank bacteria.

What actually clears it

  • A plunger for soft blockages near the fixture.
  • A hand auger for hair and soap a few feet in.
  • A cable machine for deeper or repeat clogs.
  • A camera to see what is really in the line.
  • Nothing to mix and nothing to splash.

A safe order of operations

Ask what is already in it.

Think back over the last few days. If any cleaner, acid or drain product went in, do not add anything and do not plunge until the room is ventilated. If you are unsure what went in, stop and call.

Go mechanical.

Wear gloves and eye protection. Use a plunger, then a hand auger if needed. Pull the clog out and bag it, because bleach and chemicals are not the tool for this.

Rinse and ventilate.

After the water runs freely, run plain water for a few minutes and keep the fan or a window open. Clean the basin with a single product, one at a time, never two together.

Call at the first red flag.

More than one fixture slow, gurgling from a drain, water that returns after you clear it, or a sewage smell all point to the main line. Stop using water and call.

Allendale, MI: sewer lines, lift stations and septic tanks

The township runs its own sewer system, with about 50 miles of sewer main, 8 lift stations and a Water Resource Recovery Facility rated for 1.6 million gallons a day, with an expansion under way. If several drains back up at once, the blockage may be in your own line or in the public main.

Ask the township utility where its responsibility ends, and look up its posted daytime and after-hours numbers for sewer emergencies on its water and sewer page before you need them.

Rural properties are different. Private wells and septic systems fall under the Ottawa County Health Department. If you have a tank, ask the department what your system can tolerate, because the bacteria in the tank are the whole treatment process and a jug of bleach poured into a drain is not normal household use.

Frequently asked questions

Will bleach dissolve hair in a drain?

Not reliably. Hair and soap scum sit in a clump that bleach can discolor without removing. A hand auger pulls the clump out, which is the actual fix.

Can I use bleach and a drain cleaner together?

No. Combining bleach with a drain cleaner can release toxic gas. If one product did not work, do not add a second. Ventilate the room, stop, and call a plumber.

Is bleach bad for a septic system?

Normal laundry and cleaning amounts are one thing, and dumping bleach down a drain over and over is another. Large or repeated doses can harm the bacteria a tank depends on. Ask the Ottawa County Health Department about your own system.

Why does my drain still smell after I used bleach?

Bleach masks odor for a while without removing what is making it. A lasting smell usually comes from buildup in the trap, a dry trap in an unused drain or a venting problem. Run water in unused drains, and call if the smell stays.

What is a safe way to freshen a smelly drain?

Remove the stopper or strainer and clean off the hair and film by hand with gloves. Flush with plain hot tap water, then use an enzyme maintenance product if you like. Never add a second cleaner on top.

What if I already poured bleach on top of another product?

Leave the area, get fresh air and do not plunge. Call emergency services or poison control if anyone feels ill. Then call a plumber and name both products so the right protection is used.

Who do I call in Allendale if a backup might be in the public sewer main?

Call the township utility's posted emergency number, found on its water and sewer page, to ask whether the main is involved. Call us for the part inside your property. If sewage is coming up, keep everyone out of the water and away from electrical outlets.

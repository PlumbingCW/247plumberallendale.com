Exterior Faucet Repair in Allendale, MI: A Hose Bib Guide

Most outdoor faucet problems are a worn washer or packing, and those are quick fixes. The ones that need real attention are the faucet that leaks inside the wall and the one that stopped working after a hard freeze.

Here is how to tell them apart, the safe order to shut things off, and what Allendale's winter weather means for a spigot you leave attached to a hose.

Four ways an outdoor faucet goes wrong

Start by noticing which of these you have. The cause and the urgency are very different.

Drips from the spout when it is off.

Usually a worn washer or seat. This is the common, low-urgency fault and a simple repair.

Leaks around the handle.

Water at the stem when the faucet is on points to worn packing or a loose packing nut.

Water inside the wall or ceiling.

If you find damp drywall, a stain or dripping in the basement near the faucet, the pipe behind it may be split. Treat that as urgent.

Handle will not turn, or turns freely.

A seized stem, or a stem that spins without opening or closing, means internal parts have failed. Do not force it.

The safe order when it leaks or you suspect a freeze

Work in this sequence. Skipping to the faucet itself is how a drip becomes a flood.

Take the hose off.

Disconnect and drain every hose, splitter and timer. A hose left on holds water against the faucet and is the usual cause of winter trouble.

Close the indoor shutoff.

Many outdoor faucets have their own valve inside, often in a basement or utility space where the line passes through the wall. Close it first.

Open the outdoor faucet.

With the valve closed, open the faucet to drain what is left in the line, and leave it open until you know what is wrong.

Look before you thaw.

Check the wall inside for wet spots. Never use a torch or open flame on a suspected frozen pipe. If water is already moving where it should not, call.

NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals for the Grand Rapids airport, about 16 miles east of Allendale, show 133 days a year with a low at or below 32 F. They also show 52 days at or below 20 F and about 4 days at or below 0 F. Allendale sits closer to Lake Michigan, so local numbers can differ.

That means more than a third of the year has freezing nights. Take the hose off when the first frosts arrive, not when a cold snap is forecast.

About the 42-inch frost depth you may have heard

Michigan's residential code table lists a 42-inch frost line depth, but that figure is for footings and foundations. It is not a rule for how deep water pipes are buried, and it says nothing about the exposed pipe at your outside wall. Do not assume a water line is protected just because a frost depth exists.

What a repair or replacement usually involves

A washer, seat or packing repair on a standard faucet takes a few turns of a wrench. A frost-proof faucet has a long stem that reaches inside the heated space, and the shutoff happens well inside the wall, which is why a worn one is trickier. If the faucet needs replacing, the connection is inside the wall, and that is work for a plumber.

A good faucet repair visit checks the interior valve, the slope of the faucet toward the outside and whether a vacuum breaker is leaking at the top. If a pipe has split, you are looking at a burst pipe repair, where the first job is stopping the water and the second is looking at what got wet.

Fall routine that avoids most spigot calls

  • Remove hoses, splitters and timers before the first hard frost..
  • Close the indoor shutoff for each outdoor faucet and open the outdoor one to drain..
  • Watch for a drip when the faucet is off. A small drip left alone gets worse..
  • Check the wall inside near each faucet in spring, when the water goes back on, and watch for damp spots..
  • Know where each indoor shutoff is, and make sure everyone in the house does too..

Frequently asked questions

Why does my outdoor faucet drip when it is turned off?

A worn washer or damaged seat is the usual cause. Close the indoor valve before you take anything apart. If a new washer does not stop it, the seat is likely pitted and the faucet needs more than a washer.

Can I repair a frost-proof faucet myself?

You can often replace a washer or packing if you can shut the water off first. Replacing the faucet itself means working on the pipe inside the wall, and that is a job for a plumber.

Why did my frost-proof faucet freeze?

These faucets only work when the water can drain out of the stem. A hose left attached traps water, and a faucet that does not slope slightly outward can hold water too. Either way, ice can crack the stem or the pipe behind it.

How do I know if the pipe inside the wall is damaged?

Open the faucet after the water is back on and look for dripping or dampness inside near it. A stain, soft drywall or a wet basement ceiling means the pipe may be split. Close the indoor valve and call.

Should I cover the faucet with an insulated cap?

A cap helps a little against wind and a light freeze, but it does not replace removing the hose and draining the line. Think of it as a second layer, not a substitute.

What will an outdoor faucet repair cost?

It depends on whether it is packing, a washer or a full replacement, how hard the interior side is to reach and the hour of the call. We give you the clear price before work starts.

How cold does it get in Allendale before I should worry about the faucet?

The Grand Rapids airport sees 133 days a year at or below 32 F, and the National Weather Service has recorded minus 19 F near Allendale in January 2024. Any night near freezing with a hose attached is a risk, so take hoses off in the fall.

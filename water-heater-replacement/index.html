Water heater failed or leaking right now? Do these first

  1. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us. Do not flip switches or hunt for the source..
  2. Close the cold water supply valve on the pipe that feeds the top of the tank. A lever or a round wheel, either one stops the refill..
  3. Cut the energy: the double breaker for an electric tank, or the gas control knob to off for a gas tank..
  4. Keep children and pets back, and stay out of standing water near outlets, power strips or the panel..
  5. Call the line and tell us the fuel, roughly how old the tank is, and whether it is dripping, pooling or spraying..

Sizing and fuel: what we match before we quote

Most households do best with a like-for-like swap: same fuel, similar capacity, similar footprint. That keeps the gas line, vent and circuit as they are and keeps the job short. We read the rating plate, then ask how many people shower in the morning.

Changing fuel is a bigger job. Gas to electric needs a proper circuit, and electric to gas needs a gas line and a vent path. If you are weighing tankless or a hybrid, water heater installation covers the planned route. A tank that quit overnight is usually best replaced like for like and upgraded later.

Venting, permits and inspection

On a gas tank the exhaust path matters most. We check the flue slope, connector, draft, gas shutoff and sediment trap before the new burner lights. Electric tanks get breaker size, wire condition and terminal checks.

In Allendale Township, plumbing and mechanical permits and inspections go through Professional Code Inspections. Michigan's plumbing code currently adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code, and your inspector can tell you which edition applies to your permit.

What decides the price of a replacement

You get a clear price before work starts. We keep dollar figures off this page because the number moves with the five things below.

Fuel and type.

Like for like gas or electric is the baseline. Changing fuel or moving to a hybrid adds circuit, gas line or vent work.

Capacity.

A bigger tank costs more to buy, takes more floor and sometimes needs a larger vent.

Venting and code items.

A corroded vent, a new expansion tank, discharge piping or a drain pan all change the parts list.

Access.

A tank in a tight utility closet or down a steep stair takes more labor than one in an open garage corner.

Damage around the tank.

A tank that leaked for days may have hurt shutoff valves, nearby piping or the floor drain, and that is priced separately.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle water heater replacement emergencies at night or on weekends in Allendale?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. For a failed tank we offer same-day visits when our schedule allows, and the person on the phone tells you what to shut off while you wait.

Can a leaking water heater be repaired instead of replaced?

It depends where the water comes from. A weeping drain valve, connector or relief valve is a repair. Water from the tank body or a seam means the tank has failed, and our water heater repair page explains how to tell the difference.

How do I find out how old my water heater is?

Look at the rating plate on the side of the tank. The manufacture date is printed or built into the serial number. Read it to us on the call and we can tell you what it means for repair or replace.

Can you switch my tank from gas to electric, or the other way?

Yes, but it is more than a swap. Gas to electric needs a proper circuit. Electric to gas needs a gas line and a vent route. We tell you what it involves before you commit.

Does a water heater replacement need a permit in Allendale Township?

Permits and inspections for plumbing and mechanical work go through Professional Code Inspections. Whether your job needs one is a question for them, and we will tell you what we expect on the call.

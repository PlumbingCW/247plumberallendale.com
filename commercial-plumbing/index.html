Commercial leak detection: finding water that is not on the floor yet

Commercial leak detection starts with the meter. Close every fixture, stop the irrigation, ice machines and any equipment on a fill valve, then watch the leak indicator or sweep hand on the meter. If it keeps moving, water is escaping somewhere after the meter. If it stops, the loss is with a specific piece of equipment.

Sub-meters make this faster. In an apartment building or a tenant space, subtract the readings of the individual sub-meters from the main meter. A gap means the loss is in the common lines, such as a riser, a boiler feed or a hidden line under the lot. We then close sections one by one and listen to narrow it down.

Backflow testing for businesses

Many commercial properties carry backflow assemblies on irrigation, boilers, fire lines or kitchen equipment. Michigan has plumbing rules on backflow assemblies, so ask your inspector or water utility which devices on your property need testing and how often.

If you got a notice about testing, call and tell us what it says. We can check the assembly and tell you what you are looking at. See plumbing installation for installing or replacing the assembly itself.

Grease trap cleaning and why FOG controls matter

Fats, oils and grease, called FOG, cool inside the drain line and build up into a plug. A grease trap catches it before it reaches the sewer, but only if it is emptied before it fills. A trap that is full sends grease downstream, backs up the kitchen and can cause a sewer backup for the neighbor.

Ask the township utility about its grease trap and FOG requirements before you decide how often to clean. Our hydro jetting work clears the lines after the trap when grease has gone past it.

Storm drain and catch basin cleaning

Parking lot catch basins and roof drains fill with leaves, silt and trash. After a hard rain or a spring thaw, a blocked one sends water to the loading dock or the lobby. We clean them out and flush the line behind them, and for a clogged interior drain line our drain cleaning crew can help.

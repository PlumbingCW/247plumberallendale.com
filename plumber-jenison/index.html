Softer Wyoming water, Grandville sewers, and what that changes in a house

Township public works describes itself as a distribution system only. Wyoming produces the water, while Grandville treats the sewage across the Kent County line. A pressure drop on your whole block points at the utility before it points at your pipes, so ask a neighbor before calling anyone.

The township's 2025 water quality report prints hardness between 89 and 108 mg/L, averaging 99.19. That is roughly 5.8 grains per gallon by our conversion, softer than the Grand Rapids-supplied water Allendale gets. A Jenison water heater collects scale more slowly as a result, and a softener set up for harder water elsewhere will use more salt than this supply needs.

Three Jenison problems, in the order we would check them

The median home in the Jenison census area dates to 1973 (1977 across ZIP 49428), and about 88% are owner-occupied, so most repairs are an owner choosing between fixing and replacing.

Sewage coming up a basement drain.

The township requires backwater valves on new homes only, and says many older homes lack one. A house is more exposed when its lowest drain sits under 24 inches above the nearest upstream manhole. Start with drain cleaning, and ask about sewer line cleaning if the clog keeps returning.

A sump pump that cycles all spring.

Township rules keep footing drains, eave troughs and sump pumps out of the sanitary sewer, and the township inspects for it. If your pump discharges anywhere but outside, sump pump work should reroute that discharge.

A water heater from the last owner.

A house built around 1973 has almost certainly been through a tank already. Rust at the base, water on the floor or a tank past its second decade points toward water heater replacement, not another repair.

Paperwork: where permits and backflow questions go

The township does not accept plumbing permits. They go straight to Professional Code Inspections, whose local office is at 1913 Baldwin St in Jenison. If your job needs a permit, file there, not at the township hall at 1515 Baldwin St.

Georgetown also runs a cross connection program. Its page lists water softeners, lawn irrigation, water-assisted sump pumps and boilers as residential examples, and Chapter 58 of the township code covers the subject. If you own any of those, ask the township what backflow protection it expects.

Pressure dips: irrigation hours and a new water main

On June 19, 2024 the township used 3,813,300 gallons between 5 and 9 a.m., driven by lawn irrigation, and it has planned a larger main from Wyoming. A weak summer-morning shower can come from demand, not from a failing valve in your house, so test a tap at midday first.

Phase 2 of a 24-inch transmission main runs April through November 2026 on 48th Ave and Baldwin St. If your pressure changes while crews work nearby, call township public works before paying for a leak search.

Frequently asked questions

Is emergency service available in Jenison?

Yes. The line is open 24/7 for Jenison, including nights, weekends and holidays, and a dispatcher sends a plumber from Allendale. Tell us the street and what the water is doing. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 first.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Jenison?

Street addresses in Jenison use 49428, which runs along Baldwin Street and Chicago Drive through Georgetown Township. 49429 is a PO Box ZIP with no street addresses. If your mail shows another ZIP, such as the Hudsonville 49426 next door, give the dispatcher your street address and we will place you.

How long does it take to reach Jenison from Allendale?

It is about 10 miles and about 15 minutes by our own estimate, without traffic. Weather and call volume change that, so we make no arrival promise.

Who do I call about a water main break in Jenison?

For a main break outside business hours, the township says to contact Ottawa County dispatch, or 911 in an emergency. Everything inside your walls is a plumber's job, so shut off the main and call our line as well.

Are any Jenison homes on wells or septic?

Possibly. The township says wells are permitted by the Ottawa County Health Department and that it keeps no well records. Where public sewer is available, connecting is mandatory, while public water is optional. Ask the township about your address.

Does my older Jenison house need a backwater valve?

The township does not require older homes to add one. Your exposure depends on how high your lowest drain sits above the nearest upstream manhole, so ask the township for that height and have a plumber measure your drain. A valve only helps if someone keeps it clear.

Can you replace a leaking water heater in Jenison the same day?

Same-day appointments are offered when a slot is available, and a leaking tank is a call we try to fit in. Shut off the cold supply to the heater first.

Toilet overflowing right now? Four moves before we get there

  1. Stop flushing, lift the tank lid and push the rubber flapper down onto its seat so no more water drops into the bowl..
  2. Close the supply valve behind the toilet by turning it clockwise until it stops. If it spins loosely or will not close, go to the next move..
  3. Shut off the main water valve for the building, usually near the meter or where the line comes through the basement wall..
  4. Keep everyone out of water that has reached outlets or power strips. If more than one drain is gurgling, tell us when you call, because that points to the main line and not just the toilet..

What a failing toilet looks and sounds like

Water rising or spilling.

The bowl fills and will not drop after a flush. A clog in the trap is the usual cause. If the tub or floor drain also gurgles, the blockage sits deeper than the toilet.

Wet floor at the base.

Water around the bottom after a flush means a failed wax ring, loose closet bolts or a cracked flange. A damp spot that returns after you wipe it is the classic pattern.

A running toilet is quiet money going down the drain

Allendale Charter Township's 2025 water quality report calls toilet leaks possibly the biggest source of water use in a home, and notes that they often go unheard until someone lifts the tank lid. Its check is simple: the water in the tank should sit about one inch below the top of the overflow tube. Anything higher can spill over that edge unnoticed.

The report also describes a flapper that seals on some flushes and not others, which leaves water running until the next flush. A few drops of food coloring in the tank, left alone for fifteen minutes, will show up in the bowl if the flapper leaks. A new flapper or fill valve is a small repair, and we say so instead of selling a new toilet.

Flange repair: repair ring or proper rebuild?

The flange is the ring on the floor that the toilet bolts to and seals against. When it cracks or sits too low, a new wax ring alone will not hold.

Repair kit or ring

  • A metal or plastic ring screws over a cracked flange and restores the bolt slots..
  • It works when the pipe beneath is sound and the flange is only chipped..
  • It cannot help a flange that is rotted loose or sitting below the floor surface..

Proper repair

  • Cut out the old flange and tie in a new one to the drain pipe with the correct fitting..
  • Check the subfloor and replace soft wood before anything is set..
  • Set the flange on top of the finished floor so the wax seals fully..

Burst or broken supply valve behind the toilet

The stop valve and braided supply line behind the bowl are the parts most likely to fail all at once. A cracked nut, a corroded stem or a line that lets go can spray water, and it is an emergency if the valve will not close. Shut off the main, then call us at any hour.

Old multi-turn valves often weep at the stem or refuse to turn after years untouched, and forcing one can snap it. We replace the valve and line together, usually with a quarter-turn valve, so the next shutoff takes one move. If the leak runs into the basement, our leak detection work covers water you cannot see.

Installing a toilet: wax ring, flange and rough-in

A new toilet is mostly about what sits under the bowl. The old wax ring comes out, the flange is checked for cracks and height, and a fresh ring and new closet bolts go in. The rough-in, meaning the distance from the wall to the flange center, decides which models fit. Twelve inches is the most common, though 10 and 14 exist.

Taller comfort-height bowls sit higher than standard ones, so mention that preference when you call. We level the bowl, snug the bolts evenly so the porcelain does not crack, then seal and test. If the work also needs a new drain or supply line, see our plumbing installation page.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle toilet emergencies at night or on weekends in Allendale?

Yes. Overflows, leaks at the base and failed supply valves go to our 24/7 line, and nights and weekends are handled the same way as weekdays. Close the valve behind the toilet or the main while you wait, and we take it from there.

How do I know if my toilet needs a new wax ring?

Water around the base after flushing, a bowl that rocks, or a sewer smell in the bathroom all point to the ring. We pull the toilet and check the flange at the same time, because a new ring over a cracked flange leaks again.

Can you fix a toilet that keeps running?

Usually the fix is a flapper, fill valve or chain, not a new toilet. We replace the part and tell you straight if the tank itself is cracked.

Can I keep using a toilet that leaks at the base?

We would not. A leaking base keeps soaking the subfloor, and a loose bowl can fail on the next flush. Close the valve, put a towel down and wait for the repair.

Do you work on toilets in rental properties?

Yes, for landlords and property managers, one toilet or several. Tell us the unit and whether a tenant will be home. Larger buildings fall under commercial plumbing.

How is a toilet clog different from a main line backup?

One slow toilet while the rest of the house drains normally is usually a local clog. Several fixtures gurgling together points to the main line, and that is a sewer line cleaning job.

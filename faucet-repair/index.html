Faucet or valve will not shut off? Do this right now

  • Close the angle stop under the sink by turning it clockwise. If it spins freely or leaks, stop forcing it..
  • Shut the main water valve, then open the lowest faucet in the house to drain the lines..
  • Put a bucket under any drip and a towel in the cabinet, because water runs along pipes and pools out of sight..
  • Leave a handle or stem that has started to spray exactly as it is. Pulling on it can make the leak worse..

Faucet, valve and fixture repair: what we fix

This page covers kitchen and bathroom faucets, laundry and utility sinks, pull-down sprayers and the shutoff valves under them. Most calls are a drip, a handle that has gone stiff, low flow from a clogged aerator, or water leaking into the cabinet below. Under-sink problems often involve the garbage disposal too, so we check both.

Many repairs come down to a cartridge, an O-ring or a seat. A discontinued finish or an unusual model may need a part ordered, and that is where replacing is often the better call. Mention the name on the handle or spout when you phone.

Repair or replace: faucet type by faucet type

Most faucets fall into four families, and the symptom usually tells you which one you own.

How a faucet visit goes

Shut and look.

We close the shutoffs and open the cabinet to see where the water really comes from. Many faucet leaks turn out to be the supply line or the valve.

Name the fix.

You hear whether it is a cartridge, a seat or the whole fixture, along with the price, before parts come out.

Repair or swap.

We rebuild the faucet or install the new one with fresh supply lines.

Test.

Hot, cold, sprayer and aerator get checked, then a dry paper towel goes under the cabinet to catch any weep.

Shower valves, showerheads and tub faucets

A showerhead that drips after shutoff often has a worn cartridge in the valve behind the wall, while a leak at the threads is usually tape or a washer. Water that falls from the tub spout while the shower runs means the diverter has worn out. A handle that turns without changing the temperature is a valve cartridge, and many can be swapped from the front.

Burst or broken supply valves and angle stops

Angle stops are the small shutoffs under sinks. Many were installed once and never turned, so when a stem finally fails, water sprays or keeps coming. A valve that weeps at the stem, drips when touched or looks green with corrosion should be replaced before it breaks.

If one has already let go, shut the main, then we replace the valve and the supply line together. If the main valve itself is stuck, tell us on the call, since that changes how we plan the visit. A split line behind a wall belongs under burst pipe repair, and after-hours calls use the same 24/7 line.

Hose bibs, outdoor faucets and winter shutoff

An outdoor faucet that drips behind the handle needs packing or a washer, and one that leaks inside the wall after winter needs a closer look. Before frost, take hoses off, close the interior shutoff to that line if there is one, and open the outdoor tap to drain it. Frost-free types still hold water in the barrel if a hose stays attached.

Allendale, MI: the township's own drip test

The Allendale Charter Township 2025 water quality report offers a way to size up a drip. Hold a glass under it for one hour, then multiply by 24 to see how much water goes down the drain in a day. Do it before you decide a drip can wait.

The same report describes checking for a hidden leak at the meter. With every faucet off, a plus sign on a newer plastic meter, or a spinning triangle on an older brass one, means water is moving. If the meter says yes and every fixture is repaired, that is when leak detection is the next step.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle faucet emergencies at night or on weekends in Allendale?

Yes. A faucet, angle stop or shower valve that will not shut off goes to our 24/7 line at any hour. Shut the main water valve while you wait, and tell us when you call if that valve is stuck.

Why does my faucet still drip after I tighten the handle?

Tightening usually makes it worse. A drip after shutoff means a worn washer, seat or cartridge. A drip also wears the valve seat, which can turn a cheap washer job into a new faucet.

My shower handle spins but the temperature will not change. What failed?

That is typically the valve cartridge or the stop inside it. Many can be replaced from the front without opening the wall. If the stem or body is damaged, we explain the options.

Can you replace the shutoff valves under my sink?

Yes, and it is a good moment to replace the supply lines too. Valves that have not been turned in years are the ones most likely to fail.

Should I shut off my outdoor faucet in winter?

Take the hose off, close the interior shutoff if there is one, and let the outdoor tap drain. A hose left on can trap water even in a frost-free faucet.

Do you repair faucets outside Allendale?

Yes, in nearby towns including Hudsonville, Jenison, Marne and Coopersville. Tell us the address when you call.

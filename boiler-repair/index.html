No heat in winter right now? What to do first

  1. Smell gas or hear a carbon monoxide alarm? Leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before anything else..
  2. Check the thermostat is set to heat and above room temperature, and that the boiler switch and breaker are on..
  3. Read the pressure gauge. A reading near zero means the system has lost water. Do not keep feeding it, because that hides a leak..
  4. If water is dripping from the relief valve or pooling at the boiler, shut off the cold fill line to the boiler and call..
  5. If the house is dropping toward freezing, tell us on the call. Pipes become the second problem, and a burst pipe is a separate emergency..

Signs your boiler is asking for help

Falling pressure.

The gauge sinks over days. Water is going somewhere, through a leak (leak detection can find it) or a failed expansion tank.

Banging or kettling.

Air, scale or sludge in the boiler makes it knock, rumble or whistle as it heats.

One cold zone.

If the others are warm, suspect a zone valve or thermostat. If all are cold, suspect the circulator pump.

Cold tops on radiators.

A radiator hot at the bottom and cold at the top usually has air trapped in it.

Dripping relief valve.

It is opening from overpressure. A failed expansion tank or a bad fill valve is often behind it.

Pumps, zone valves, radiators and baseboard

Hot water heat moves in a loop. A circulator pump pushes water from the boiler through radiators or baseboard and back. Zone valves open and close each area on thermostat command. When the boiler is hot but the house is cold, the fault is usually the pump, a stuck zone valve or air locked in the loop.

Bleeding a radiator vent can fix cold tops, but the water inside is hot, so be careful and have a rag ready. Baseboard loops trap air differently and are harder to clear. If bleeding does not help, or the pressure drops again afterward, call us instead of repeating it.

Where we stop, and where we step in

We are plumbers, so we are honest about limits. Gas burner and combustion problems may need a heating specialist. If only the hot water failed, see water heater repair.

What affects the cost of a boiler repair

We quote a clear price before work starts, and keep dollar figures off this page. A same-day or after-hours visit is priced as we tell you on the call.

Which part failed.

A fill valve or air vent is a smaller repair than a circulator pump or zone valve.

Number of zones.

More zones means more valves, thermostats and loops to test.

Type of heat.

Cast iron radiators and baseboard behave differently and bleed differently.

Age and parts availability.

Older boilers may need hard-to-find parts, which affects repair or replace advice.

Water loss.

If pressure keeps dropping, finding the leak can add work before the boiler is fixed.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle boiler repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Allendale?

Yes. The line is open 24/7 and we take no-heat calls on nights, weekends and holidays. Same-day visits are offered when available, and you are told what to protect while you wait.

My boiler pressure gauge reads zero. What does that mean?

The system has lost water. Do not keep refilling it, since that can hide a leak and add fresh oxygen. Call us and we will look for the loss.

Is a dripping relief valve dangerous?

It is a warning that pressure is too high. A failed expansion tank or fill valve is a common cause. Do not cap it. Call us.

Why are the tops of my radiators cold?

Air is trapped. Bleeding the vent can help, but hot water comes out, so go slowly. If it keeps happening, the system is taking in air or losing water.

Do you repair the burner and combustion side?

We handle the plumbing and hydronic side. If the problem is the burner or combustion, we tell you and you may need a heating specialist. Gas smell means leave first.

What can I do to keep pipes safe if the heat is out?

Keep interior doors open, open cabinets under sinks on outside walls and keep the warmest room closed in. If a pipe freezes or bursts, call right away.

A well changes the first ten minutes of an emergency

On a well, the pressure tank keeps pushing water into a split pipe after you close a valve. Shut the valve past the tank, then switch off the well pump breaker so the tank cannot refill the line.

Ottawa County advises testing a private well every year for coliform, nitrates, total dissolved solids and pH, and notes that natural contaminants such as arsenic, radon and uranium, and nearby septic systems, can affect well water. We found no hardness figure for Marne, so test your tap. If a filter or softener follows, water softener and filtration work should start from that result.

Three calls that come from a mixed-utility address

Pressure that rises and falls.

On a well, a pump that cycles often is a classic sign of a hidden leak or a waterlogged tank. On township water, the meter tells the story. Leak detection begins by working out which system you have.

Sewage backing up.

On septic, a backup can mean a full tank rather than a clogged line, and that is a septic pumper's job. On sewer billed through Grand Rapids it is a line clog and ours. Tell us about septic before we start.

A heater past its years.

About 28% of occupied homes in Wright Township use utility gas, against 82% in Tallmadge (ACS 2019 to 2023). So the first question is often fuel, then size. The median build year for ZIP 49435 is 1976. See water heater replacement.

Frequently asked questions

Is emergency service available in Marne?

Yes. The line is open 24/7 for Marne, nights and weekends included, and a plumber is sent from Allendale. Tell the dispatcher about any well or septic system when you call. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Marne?

Yes. 49435 is the Marne ZIP and covers the village, central Tallmadge Township and parts of Wright. 49404, the Coopersville ZIP, also serves northwest Tallmadge and part of Wright, and Wright Township's own office has a 49404 address. We dispatch by street address, so either ZIP gets the same 24/7 response from our line.

How far is Marne from your Allendale office?

About 9 miles and about 15 minutes by our estimate, northeast along M-45 and 24th Avenue. Rural roads and weather change that, and we do not promise an arrival time.

Who is my water utility in Marne?

There is no village water utility. Wright Township lists sewer billing through the City of Grand Rapids for some properties and sends well and septic permits to the county. Tallmadge Township takes water and sewer hook-up applications. The townships can tell you which applies at your address.

Is a well pump problem the same as a plumbing leak?

They overlap. A pump that cycles every few minutes can mean a leak somewhere in the house, or a tank that has lost its air charge. We check for the leak first.

Are all Marne homes on well and septic?

No, and we will not guess. Wright Township lists Grand Rapids sewer billing for some properties, and Tallmadge has township water and sewer, so the area is a mix. Ask the township or the county health department about your address.

Which township office handles plumbing permits near Marne?

Both Wright and Tallmadge send plumbing permits to Professional Code Inspections, with township-specific forms. Tallmadge's forms page says that office took over all township permits and inspections as of September 1, 2013.

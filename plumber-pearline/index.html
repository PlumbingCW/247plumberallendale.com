Pearline is the old name for the stretch of Lake Michigan Drive about a mile east of central Allendale, and many neighbors still call it East Allendale. It has no official borders, so this page is for homes along that corridor and the streets that feed it.

What Pearline is, and what it is not

Wikipedia describes Pearline, also known as East Allendale, as an unincorporated community about one mile east of Allendale on M-45, which is Lake Michigan Drive. A rural post office named Pearline operated there from April 1891 to May 1908, and its first postmaster was a fruit grower named Everhart.

The Census Bureau does not publish Pearline as a place, and we found no township map that draws its edges. Instead of guessing a boundary, we describe it by anchors. Lake Michigan Drive runs through it, and 56th Avenue is one of the north-south roads where homes are being built.

Pearline is inside Allendale Charter Township, so its permits, water and sewer questions go to the same township offices as the rest of Allendale. It is not a separate town.

A newer housing mix, with one caution

The Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 estimates for the whole township put the median home at 2003, with about 32% of homes built since 2010 and about 16% before 1980. Those numbers describe the township, not Pearline alone, and we cannot split them out for one unmapped area.

What that does suggest is that many nearby homes are not old enough for the age-related failures that dominate older cities. The usual calls on newer houses are a worn water heater, a leaking fixture, a clog or a softener that needs attention. We do not assume any particular defect in your house.

Three questions to settle before a plumber arrives

Answering these first makes the call shorter and the diagnosis faster.

Is the house on township sewer or septic?.

Township sewer reaches only part of Allendale. The Master Plan says municipal service will not cover the whole township in any foreseeable period. A recurring backup means very different things on each system, so check a utility bill or ask the township. Drain cleaning starts with that answer.

Is the water from the township or a private well?.

Township water is bought from the City of Grand Rapids. Homes outside the water district use private wells installed under the Ottawa County Health Department. Hardness, pressure and softener needs differ, so mention which one you have. See water softener and filtration.

How old is the water heater?.

A tank installed with a new house eventually needs a decision between repair and replacement. Look for the date on the rating label, then read about water heater repair or replacement before you call.

Hard township water and what it leaves behind

The Grand Rapids water system's 2025 average analysis lists hardness of 142 mg/L, or about 8.3 grains per gallon. On the USGS scale, 121 to 180 mg/L counts as hard. White crust on faucet aerators and shower heads, and scale inside a tank water heater, are the visible results.

Private wells vary widely and the Grand Rapids figure does not apply to them. If your water comes from a well, a lab test is the only way to know what you have.

Sources: Grand Rapids Water System 2025 average water analysis, USGS water hardness scale, Allendale Charter Township Master Plan.

New streets near Lake Michigan Drive

Builders have been active east of the township center. Pearline Estates advertises extra-large home sites on 56th Avenue, and Eastbrook Homes announced Emerald Springs between 60th and 64th Avenues near Lake Michigan Drive. We mention them as nearby developments, not as parts of Pearline, because no source assigns them to it.

If you live in one of these newer streets, the plumbing questions are usually about fixtures, water heaters and sump pumps rather than aging lines. A sump pump check is worth doing in any house with a below-grade level, whatever its age.

Between town and campus

Pearline lies between central Allendale and the Grand Valley State University campus. Rentals mix with owner-occupied houses on this side of the township, and landlords and tenants will find a separate page for the GVSU campus area. For the rest of the township, start at the Allendale page.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pearline the same as East Allendale?

Yes. Wikipedia lists East Allendale as another name for Pearline, an unincorporated community about a mile east of Allendale on Lake Michigan Drive. Both are inside Allendale Charter Township.

Which ZIP code does Pearline use?

Mail for the township is overwhelmingly 49401, the Allendale ZIP. A small area in the southeast corner uses 49428 and another on the south edge uses 49426, according to Wikipedia. Check your own mailing address.

Does Pearline have municipal sewer?

Some of the township does and some does not. The township says it will not extend sewer and water everywhere in any foreseeable period. Your utility bill or the township utility office at 616-895-6295 can confirm your address.

Do I need a permit for plumbing work in Pearline?

Many plumbing jobs do. Allendale Township sends permit and inspection requests to Professional Code Inspections at 616-667-8803. Ask before work starts, and ask your plumber who pulls the permit.

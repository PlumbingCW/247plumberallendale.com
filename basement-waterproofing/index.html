Basement Waterproofing in Allendale, MI

Water in a basement comes from two places: outside the walls, or from a pipe. The fix is different for each, so the first job is to figure out which one you have. A plumber handles the pipe side and the pump side, and we will be clear about what falls outside that.

Wall seepage or a plumbing leak? How to tell the two apart

Looks like groundwater

  • Dampness along the base of the wall or where the floor meets the wall..
  • Water that shows up after rain or snowmelt and dries out in a dry week..
  • White powdery mineral deposits on masonry..
  • A damp patch with no pipe anywhere near it..

Looks like a plumbing leak

  • Water that appears with no rain at all, or in a dry spell..
  • A wet spot near a pipe, water heater, washer or a floor drain..
  • A water meter that moves with every fixture off..
  • A stain below a bathroom, or a drip from the joists above..

The simple test that separates them

Close the main valve for a few hours and see if the dampness changes. If the wet spot stays the same or grows, water is coming from outside. If it dries, a pipe was the source. Our leak detection page covers the meter test that goes along with it. A pump that is struggling is covered under sump pump repair.

Taping a square of plastic sheet to the wall for a day is an old trick. Moisture under the plastic means it came through the wall. Moisture on top means it is condensation from room air.

Interior drain tile and the sump pit

Interior drain tile is a perforated pipe laid along the inside edge of the basement floor, below the slab level. Water that gets under the slab or along the footing flows into the tile and runs to a sump pit, where a pump sends it out of the house.

It deals with water pressure under the floor and at the wall base. It does not stop water from entering the soil outside. The pit and pump become the heart of the system, so a reliable pump, a check valve and a backup matter as much as the tile.

What a plumber can and cannot do

We can install or repair a sump pit and pump, add a backup, find and fix plumbing leaks, clear drain lines and floor drains, and route the discharge. A floor drain that backs up is a drain cleaning job. We can tell you when the water is coming from a pipe, and we serve Hudsonville homes too.

We do not regrade a yard, replace gutters or reseal foundation cracks. Those jobs belong to a landscaper, a gutter installer and a foundation or masonry contractor. Often the cheapest fix is on the outside: downspouts that discharge several feet from the wall, soil that slopes away from the house, and gutters that are not clogged.

Allendale, MI: young foundations, but check the lots near the river

Because Allendale housing is young, most foundations are newer than in neighboring towns, and we do not say Allendale basements leak. The median home was built in 2003, and about 32 percent of units are from 2010 or later.

Location still matters. The Grand River forms the township's northern and eastern boundaries and, per the township Master Plan, much adjacent land is in the FEMA 100-year floodplain, with wetlands in the Bass River basin in the northwest. If your lot is near the river or the wetlands, a high water table is plausible. Test with a flood map and ask the county or township about your parcel.

What decides the cost of a waterproofing job

A pit and pump swap is small, and a full interior drain tile run along every wall is big. These factors set the price.

Length of drain tile.

The more wall perimeter that needs tile, the more concrete to cut and the more days.

Slab removal and replacement.

Cutting and repouring floor along the wall is a large part of the job.

Pit and pump.

A new basin, pump, check valve and backup add parts and labor.

Discharge route.

A longer or buried line costs more, and MISS DIG 811 comes first.

Finished space.

Carpet, drywall and framing in a finished basement add work.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if the water is from a pipe or from outside?

A pipe leak shows up in dry weather and near plumbing. Groundwater shows up after rain or snowmelt and along the base of the walls. A meter test with everything off also helps.

What is interior drain tile?

A perforated pipe set along the inside perimeter below floor level that carries water to a sump pit. It manages water pressure beneath the slab. It does not keep water out of the soil outside.

Can a plumber seal foundation cracks?

Generally no. Foundation cracks are the job of a foundation or masonry contractor. We handle pipes, drains, pits and pumps, and we will tell you which one your problem is.

Are Allendale basements prone to leaking?

We do not make that claim. The housing is relatively young, so foundations are mostly newer. Lots close to the Grand River or the wetlands deserve a check on the FEMA flood map.

What can I fix outside before spending money inside?

Clean the gutters, extend the downspouts several feet from the wall, and check that the soil slopes away from the house. Many damp basements improve with those steps alone.

Do I need a sump pump with a drain tile system?

Yes, in most cases. The tile gathers the water and the sump pit and pump remove it. If the pump fails, the system backs up, so a check valve and battery backup are worth discussing.

My basement is wet right now. Who do I call?

The 24/7 line. If a pipe is running or a pump has stopped, treat it as urgent. We help with the plumbing side right away, and we say plainly if the source is outside our scope.

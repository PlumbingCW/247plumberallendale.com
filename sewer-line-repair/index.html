Clean it again, or repair it?

A sewer line cleaning fixes a blockage. A repair fixes the pipe. The signs below tell us which one you need.

A cleaning may be enough when

  • It is the first backup in years..
  • The camera shows grease, wipes or a soft clog and no damage..
  • The pipe is sound, with no offsets or cracks..
  • Scale or roots are light and the joints hold..

Repair is the better call when

  • The line backs up again within weeks of a cleaning..
  • The camera shows a crack, a hole, a sag or an offset joint..
  • Roots enter at the same joint every time..
  • A section has collapsed and the cable cannot pass..

Pipe lining and trenchless repair: what it is and where it stops

Lining pushes a resin-soaked sleeve into the existing pipe through a small access point. The sleeve is pressed against the wall and cured, forming a new pipe inside the old one. It leaves your lawn, driveway or patio mostly intact.

It fits when the pipe is cracked, rooted or leaking at joints but still holds its round shape and its slope. It does not straighten a collapse, remove a belly or bridge a missing section. Those need a dig, and we will say so.

A lined pipe has a slightly smaller bore, and the pipe still has to be cleaned and cleared beforehand. We tell you how a lining would affect your case before you commit.

Cast iron pipe repair in older homes nearby

Cast iron was widely used for house drains in older construction. Allendale is young (median year built 2003), with only about 1,368 homes built before 1980 plus some rural farmhouses. Towns nearby skew older: Jenison's CDP median is 1973, Marne's 1976 and Hudsonville's 1982.

Cast iron fails from the inside, with rust scale that narrows the pipe, or from the outside, with cracks and leaking joints. Signs include slow drains throughout, sewer smell, rust staining at fittings and flaking when pipe is tapped. Not every older house has cast iron, so we check rather than assume.

Repair can be a section swapped for plastic pipe, or a lining if the shape holds. Full replacement is considered where the whole run is thin. If you own an older rental in Marne, a camera look before buying is cheaper than a surprise later.

Drain pipe repair inside the house

A broken drain pipe under a slab or inside a wall is a smaller version of the same problem. A crack at a joint or a hole from an old nail shows up as a damp patch, a smell or a slow drain that clears and returns. We find it with a camera and, where the pipe is hidden, with leak detection before opening the floor.

Before anyone digs: MISS DIG 811 and permits

Anyone digging must call MISS DIG 811 at least 3 business days before. The call is free, applies to any project regardless of size, and gets public utility lines marked. Private lines, such as your sewer lateral, a sump discharge, irrigation or yard lighting wire, are not marked. That is why the camera and a line trace matter for your own pipe. If a new water line is part of the dig, see water service line repair.

In the township, plumbing permits and inspections go through Professional Code Inspections. Ask us and the inspector whether your job needs a permit. Michigan's plumbing code currently adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code, so ask your inspector which edition applies to your permit.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle sewer line repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Allendale?

Yes, we are on call 24/7. At night we clear the line so you can stop the flooding, then plan the repair in daylight once the camera shows what failed.

How do I know whether I need repair instead of cleaning?

Repeated backups, a cable that stops at the same point, or camera pictures of cracks, belly or offset joints point to repair. A one-time clog with a sound pipe is a cleaning.

Can every sewer line be lined?

No. Lining needs a pipe that is still round and sloped correctly. A collapsed or offset section or a belly needs a dig.

Do I need a permit for sewer line repair?

Plumbing permits go through Professional Code Inspections in the township. Ask whether your job needs one, and we handle the questions about the work.

Who marks the lines before digging?

MISS DIG 811 marks public utilities when called 3 business days ahead, and it is free. Private lines like your own sewer lateral are not marked.

Will my yard be torn up?

A spot dig opens a narrow trench around the damaged section. Lining uses small access holes. We explain the footprint and restoration before the price.

How do I check that a plumber is licensed?

Michigan LARA has a Find or Verify a Licensed Professional page where you can look up any plumber, and you should do that with any contractor.

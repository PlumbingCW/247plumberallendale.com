Signs the clog is bigger than one fixture

Gurgling after a flush.

Air is being dragged past a partial blockage. The toilet and the nearest tub or sink usually share the same branch.

Washer drain spits into the tub.

Water has nowhere else to go, so it takes the lowest open path. That points below the washer standpipe.

Several drains slow at once.

One shared line is restricted somewhere downstream. A plunger on a single fixture will not fix this.

Floor drain smells or burbles.

A basement floor drain is often the first place a main-line clog shows itself.

A clogged drain: what is usually stuck and how it comes out

Hair and soap scum build up in bath and shower lines. Grease and food collect in kitchen lines. Wipes, paper towels and too much toilet paper catch in toilet lines. Further down, roots or mineral scale narrow the pipe from the outside in, and that kind of clog returns unless the cause is dealt with.

On arrival we ask which fixtures are affected and which one slowed first, because the order points to the branch. Then we pull the trap or open a cleanout if the layout allows it. Seeing what is in the line beats guessing, and a plain trap clog is a short job. A clog out in the buried run takes a machine, and we say which one before it goes in.

If the line clears and then stops again within days, we say so plainly. That pattern means something is wrong with the pipe itself, such as a sag or a cracked joint, and another pass with the same tool just resets the clock.

Snaking and cabling are not the same job

On the phone people use the two words as one, but the equipment differs. A hand or drum snake is a thin coiled steel line with a hook or auger head. It suits the 1.5 to 2 inch lines under sinks, tubs and showers, where it can turn tight bends.

A drain cable machine is a heavier cable on a motorized drum, fed through a cleanout into 3 and 4 inch lines. Cutter heads are chosen to the pipe size, and the power turns through roots and packed grease that a hand snake would only poke a hole in.

Which one we bring depends on what you describe on the phone, and we carry both. Both cut a channel and pull debris out. Neither scrubs the whole pipe wall, so a greasy line can narrow again, and hydro jetting washes the wall clean. A cable that stops at the same distance every time tells us the pipe is damaged at that spot.

When store-bought drain cleaner is a bad idea

Pouring a chemical first

  • Standing water in the bowl: the product sits there and can splash back on whoever works on the drain next..
  • A fully blocked pipe: the liquid pools above the clog and has nowhere to go..
  • Roots or a damaged pipe: chemicals do nothing to either..
  • On septic or very old pipe: ask a plumber before pouring anything..
  • Mixing two products: never. The fumes can injure you..

Calling before you pour

  • Tell us what you used and when, so we protect our hands and eyes when we open the trap..
  • A cable or snake removes the blockage instead of dissolving a channel through it..
  • If the line keeps clogging, a camera shows why..

Kitchen sink drain clogs

Kitchen lines clog on grease, and hot tap water does not fix it. Fat melts, runs a few feet downstream, cools against the cold pipe wall and sets. Layer after layer builds until coffee grounds, rice and eggshell catch on it and the sink drains slowly, then stops.

A kitchen sink that backs up into the laundry sink tells us the blockage is past the point where the two lines join. A disposal that hums while the sink stays full may have a jam, which is a different repair. See garbage disposal repair for that.

After clearing the line we check the dishwasher drain hose and air gap if one is fitted, since a dishwasher dumping into a half-blocked line can overflow onto the counter.

To slow the next clog, scrape plates into the trash, wipe greasy pans with a paper towel before washing, and keep fibrous scraps like celery and potato peels out of the disposal. Those habits matter more than any product from the store.

Shower and tub clogs, and drain line leaks

A slow tub is nearly always hair and soap wrapped around the stopper or in the trap just below. Lift the strainer and look before calling. If water also rises in the tub when a toilet flushes or the washer empties, the stoppage is in the line below, not the tub.

A leaking drain line behaves differently: it drips only while water is running, so a stain on the ceiling below a bathroom may grow after showers and dry in between. Finding the joint is a job for leak detection before anyone opens a wall.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle drain cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Allendale?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, and a drain backing up at 2 a.m. gets the same cable machines as a weekday call. Stop running water, tell us which fixtures are affected, and we will give you the price before work starts.

How can I tell a fixture clog from a main line clog?

If only one sink or tub is slow, the clog is probably in its own branch. If the lowest toilet gurgles, the basement floor drain smells or several fixtures slow together, the main line is involved. That one is covered under sewer line cleaning.

Is it safe to pour drain cleaner before you arrive?

Better not. Chemicals sit in a fully blocked pipe and can splash on the person opening the trap. If you already used some, tell us the brand and how long ago, so we handle the water with gloves and eye protection.

Why does my kitchen sink keep clogging every few weeks?

Usually grease coating the pipe wall, which a snake pokes through without removing. A cleaning with a different tool, or a camera look if it keeps returning, finds out whether the pipe is sagging or scaled.

Who calls you in a rental, the tenant or the landlord?

Either can call. Who pays depends on the lease and on what we find, and we describe the cause in plain terms, such as grease, wipes or roots, so the two of you can sort it out.

Will snaking damage my pipes?

A properly sized cable or snake does not hurt sound pipe. On old, cracked or collapsed pipe a cable can catch or stop, and we say so rather than force it, then suggest a camera look.

Call us, day or night

We run on the phone because plumbing problems are easier to sort out in a two-minute conversation than in a form that sits in an inbox. Use the call button on any page, or dial the number shown at the top of this one.

Active leaks, sewage backups and a water heater that is flooding the floor go to the front of the line. Slower jobs such as a dripping faucet or a planned fixture swap are welcome too, and we book them for the next available same-day or next-day opening.

What to have ready when you call

A little detail up front lets the person on the phone give you a useful first step and a realistic idea of what the visit involves.

  • The street address and the nearest cross street, in Allendale or the town you are in..
  • What you see or hear: water on the floor, a gurgling drain, no hot water, a running toilet..
  • Where the main water shutoff is, if you know, and whether you have already closed it..
  • Whether the problem is one fixture or the whole house..
  • The age of the water heater or the pipes if you know it, since age often decides repair versus replace..
